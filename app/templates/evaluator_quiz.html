Engine 1: Time-Series & Microeconomic Optimization
7-Day APMC Price Trajectory & Cold Storage Payoff Optimization

1. Momentum & Volatility Trajectory Formula

Daily projected price $\hat{P}_t$ for day $t \in [1, 7]$ is computed using directional drift momentum calibrated by commodity-specific standard deviation $\sigma_i$:

$$\hat{P}_t = P_{\text{modal}} \times \left(1 + \mu \cdot t\right) + \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_i \cdot \sqrt{t})$$

Confidence Interval Bounds:
P_min(t) = \hat{P}_t \times (1.0 - \sigma_i)
P_max(t) = \hat{P}_t \times (1.0 + \sigma_i)
Where $\mu = +1.8\%$ (Upward trend), $-1.5\%$ (Downward trend), or $+0.8\%$ (Stable).
2. Cold Storage Arbitrage Decision Rule

Computes net profitability of holding produce for 10 days in cold storage versus immediate distress sale at current mandi rate:

$$\Delta R_{\text{net}} = (\hat{P}_{t=10} - P_{\text{current}}) - (C_{\text{storage}} \times 10) - (P_{\text{current}} \times \delta_{\text{decay}})$$

Decision Threshold:
IF (Net Gain / Current Price ≥ 5.0%) AND (Storage Potential == TRUE):
  → 🟢 "HOLD & STORE (Cold Storage Recommended)"
ELSE:
  → 🟠 "SELL NOW (Direct Farm-Gate Handover)"
