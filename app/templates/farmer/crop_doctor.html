Early Blight (Alternaria solani)

टमाटर अगेती झुलसा रोग
Severity Level: Moderate
AI Diagnostic Confidence: 94%
Urgency Advisory: Action recommended within 48 hours to prevent spread.
Symptoms Observed
  • Concentric dark brown rings with target-board pattern on leaves
  • Yellow halo surrounding necrotic spots
Recommended Biological & Eco-Friendly Control:

Apply Neem oil spray (5ml/L) combined with Trichoderma viride biopesticide. Remove heavily infected lower leaves.

CIBRC-Approved Chemical Fungicide & Dilution:

Foliar spray of Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5g per litre of water OR Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2g per litre.

Long-Term Farm Hygiene:
  • Avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation to keep foliage dry.
  • Practice 3-year crop rotation with non-solanaceous crops.
