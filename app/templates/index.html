How Krishi Kendra Works

A streamlined, transparent 4-step workflow for both Farmers and Buyers

For Farmers
1
List Harvest Produce: Enter available crop quantity, expected price, quality grade, and photos.
2
Receive Direct Buyer Requests: Get notified instantly when nearby buyers match your crop and quantity range.
3
Smart Counter-Offer & Negotiation: Compare buyer offers with live Mandi benchmarks before accepting or countering.
4
Logistics & Escrow Payout: Book vehicle transport and receive guaranteed payment directly upon delivery.
For Buyers
1
Post Exact Quantity Requirement: Enter the exact kilograms or quintals needed (no bulk/small restrictions).
2
Automated Matching Engine: The system identifies and notifies relevant farmers with adequate inventory.
3
Side-by-Side Offer Comparison: Compare quotes on price, farmer rating, proximity, and quality grades.
4
Live Delivery Tracking: Track the assigned vehicle from farm gate to your warehouse/storefront.
