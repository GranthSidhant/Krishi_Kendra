5. How We Guarantee Price Accuracy & Real-World Grounding

Rates on Krishi Kendra are not hypothetical. Our 4-Tier Hybrid Price Engine anchors every quote to official government data and active trade equilibrium.

Tier 1: Agmarknet & APMC Feeds
Daily Modal Price Ingestion

Synchronized with the Ministry of Agriculture's Agmarknet API, capturing minimum, maximum, and modal price points across 3,000+ regulated mandis.

Tier 2: MSP Statutory Safeguard
MSP Floor Protection

For statutory crops (Wheat, Paddy, Mustard, Cotton), the system enforces Minimum Support Price boundaries, flagging exploitative buyer bids automatically.

Tier 3: Dynamic Elasticity
Arrival Volume & Decay Curves

Tailored commodity profiles account for perishability (e.g., Tomato vs. Grain) and arrival volume elasticity to compute 7-day and 30-day forecast curves.

Tier 4: Closed-Loop Calibration
Realized Trade Feedback

Accepted Escrow contract values continuously refine local district price indexes, reflecting true buyer willingness to pay at the farm gate.

6. End-to-End Closed-Loop Trade Lifecycle

A seamless, zero-friction path from voice-enabled listing to guaranteed bank deposit in 5 verifiable steps.

1
Vernacular Voice & AI Discovery

Farmer speaks in regional dialect (Hindi, Marathi, Telugu). Speech-to-Intent AI extracts crop, quantity, and grade automatically.

2
AI Deal Fairness Analysis

Buyer submits counter-offer. AI compares it to the live APMC index and alerts the farmer if the bid is below fair market value.

3
Upfront Escrow Lock

Buyer deposits 100% order amount into secure escrow before truck dispatch. Farmer is guaranteed risk-free payment.

4
Farm-Gate Dispatch & OTP

Transporter arrives at the farm, verifies produce quality, and logs the secure Pickup OTP to initiate GPS transit tracking.

5
Delivery OTP & Instant Payout

Buyer enters Delivery OTP upon weighing at destination. Escrow automatically releases full payout directly to farmer's UPI/bank.
