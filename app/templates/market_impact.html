AI Deal Fairness Analysis

Buyer submits counter-offer. AI compares it to the live APMC index and alerts the farmer if the bid is below fair market value.

Buyer deposits 100% order amount into secure escrow before truck dispatch. Farmer is guaranteed risk-free payment.
